# Wilds Emission System## Milestone / Gateway Plan_Certified architecture checkpoint and forward implementation gates_## Current checkpoint**Gates 1 through 6 are certified. Gate 7 is next.**The certified route now covers:```textconsumer-owned value        |        vbase emission scalar        |        +---- current Neighbor topology        |              |        |              v        |       topology scalar        |              |        +--------------+        |        vsupplied composition policy        |        vresolved emission        |        vimmutable scalar or vector carrier        |        vnormalization for vector propagation        |        vrelationship propagation        |        vreceived propagated fact        |        vimmutable eligibility decision```Consumer vocabulary remains consumer-owned. Wilds reasons over identity, topology, scalar resolution, composition, grouping, normalization, propagation, eligibility, and eventually resolved state operations without learning the consumer meaning of names or values.## Certified gateways### Gate 1 - Emitter primitive**CERTIFIED**Consumer properties and named groups resolve into finite base emission scalars while the emitter remains an ordinary Wilds object with ordinary Neighbor topology. Exact source identity, arbitrary vocabulary, and source safety are preserved.### Gate 2A - Topology scalar**CERTIFIED**Current topology can be selected through existing Neighbor sequence operators and reduced through existing scalar operators. Membership changes affect subsequent evaluation without creating a second topology system.### Gate 2B - Composition**CERTIFIED**Base and topology scalars combine through supplied `IEmissionComposition` behavior. Wilds does not hard-code addition, multiplication, weighting, normalization, or curves as universal emission semantics.### Gate 3 - Complete resolved emission**CERTIFIED**Consumer value, current topology, topology scalar reduction, and supplied composition resolve through one source-safe operation. The resulting scalar is authoritative, and emission resolution does not mutate source topology.### Gate 4 - Resolved emission carrier**CERTIFIED**An immutable carrier preserves the exact emitter, arbitrary consumer-defined emission name, and finite resolved magnitude. Carrier state remains stable after later topology changes.### Gate 5 - Propagation handoff and vector carriers**CERTIFIED**Direct scalar propagation consumes the resolved carrier without recalculation and without claiming normalization. Arbitrary N-dimensional same-source emissions may be grouped into an immutable resolved vector, normalized into an immutable normalized vector, and propagated into an immutable propagated vector.Gate 5 guarantees:- exact emitter, vector, component, and relationship references;- ordered arbitrary-N channel identity;- unique consumer-defined channel names inside one vector;- rejection of mixed-source vectors;- preservation of resolved, normalized, and received magnitudes;- normalized-vector total of approximately one;- received-vector total consistent with relationship attenuation;- immutable collection snapshots;- frozen propagation distance for historical inspection;- stability after live topology replacement.### Gate 6 - Receiver eligibility**CERTIFIED**Scalar and vector eligibility evaluate the exact received propagated `Value`. Eligibility does not substitute the resolved or normalized magnitude. Every vector component receives an independent immutable decision, and eligible and ineligible channels both remain authoritative.Gate 6 guarantees:- inclusive exact-threshold behavior;- zero, partial, and all-component eligibility states;- arbitrary N-dimensional eligibility;- exact dimension and component-order preservation;- immutable decision snapshots;- `EligibleCount`, `AnyEligible`, and `AllEligible` as descriptive summaries only;- complete Gate 5 provenance continuity;- scalar and vector decisions remain stable after live topology changes;- no consumer interpretation and no receiver-state mutation.`IsEligible` means only that the exact received fact qualified under the supplied eligibility policy at evaluation time. It does not mean that the receiver accepted the value, that an effect must occur, or that state changed.## Certification evidenceThe latest checkpoint demonstrated:- focused emitter Gates 1-6: 67/67 before the final topology-snapshot additions;- focused Gate 6 suite after snapshot additions: 13/13;- full Core suite serialized: 165/165;- isolated Wilds/QSL end-to-end repetition: 20/20;- QSL core through Wilds: 156/156 with zero missing dependencies.One prior parallel full-suite run produced one intermittent Wilds/QSL end-to-end failure.
