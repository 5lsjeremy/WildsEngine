# H4 + H4S2 Design

## H4A — Recognized Geometry Population
Immutable scoped population of exact H3F recognition results. No averaging, ranking, clustering, Truth, or Consequence projection.

## H4S2A — Narrative surface requirements
Every H4 input is classified as Derived, Supplied Definition, Supplied Policy, Supplied Metric, Supplied Service, Option, or Result. Missing supplied endpoints block the corresponding H4 implementation.

## H4S2B — Narrative Profile
Designer-facing, strongly typed narrative profile. It composes relationship definitions, composition policy, optional similarity/separation metric, profile identity/version/scope, and metadata where required. Meaning remains consumer-owned.

## H4B — Narrative Composition
Consumes H4A populations plus a registered H4S2 narrative profile. Produces multiple attributable structures. It must not force a single average or semantic opposition not supplied by the consumer.

## H4S2C — Relationship Definitions
Designer supplies semantic relationships that geometry alone cannot establish. Definitions are versioned/registered and provenance-visible.

## H4C — Competing Narrative Result
Returns the source population, distinct compositions, outliers/unassigned members, relevant relationship definitions, policy/metric identities, registration identity, and evaluation time.

## H4S2D — Consumer result/query surface
Expose registration, population composition, result retrieval/query, and provenance through WildsDiscoveries.Narratives. Internal catalogs, factories, and processors remain hidden.

## Certification rules
- Distinct recognized structures survive composition.
- No averaging unless explicitly supplied by consumer policy.
- No semantic labels/relationships are inferred from geometry alone.
- Foreign scope/profile/registration members fail.
- Supplied definition/policy/metric identities survive results.
- H3 recognition provenance survives through H4 output.
- Core remains unchanged.
