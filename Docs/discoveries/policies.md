# Wilds.Discoveries Policy Authoring

Policies are where consumer meaning belongs.

## Evidence contribution

`IEvidenceContributionPolicy` decides whether validated Evidence contributes to a Fact after Discoveries has enforced structural requirements such as exact ownership and proof identity.

Policies may inspect consumer-visible Evidence and Proof information. Useful reusable policy categories include truth/believability, strength, conditions, outcome, source, neighbors, and logical composition.

## Same Evidence, different standards

Two Facts may retain the same historical Evidence while different consumer policies produce different resolution outcomes. This is intentional. The Evidence is not rewritten to justify the policy.

## Truth congealment

Truth policy decides whether relevant narratives, clusters, and the authoritative vector series can congeal and what Truth is produced. Discoveries does not supply universal semantic Truth rules.

## Consequence projection

Consequence policy receives congealed Truth and may project zero or more Consequences. A successfully congealed Truth does not require a non-empty consequence set.

## Authoring rule

Keep semantic choices explicit. Use mechanical conveniences only where they do not obscure identity, scope, provenance, history, or meaning.
