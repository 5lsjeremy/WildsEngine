# Wilds.Discoveries Knowledge Model

## Consumer Reality
The consumer remains authoritative about the world. Discoveries does not automatically promote its own scoped Truths into Consumer Reality.

## Evidence
Evidence is attributable, entity-owned information admitted into the epistemic process. Observation and authorization describe admission; they are not separate canonical knowledge stages.

## Proof
Proof validates exact Evidence. Truth and believability can remain independent conclusions.

## Facts
Facts resolve from policy-accepted Evidence. Historical retention and current contribution are different concerns.

## Discoveries
Discoveries resolve from proven Facts while preserving local ownership. Two entities may develop different knowledge from similar information because policy remains consumer-owned.

## Geometry
Geometry represents consumer-defined knowledge dimensions. Partial knowledge may have position and motion without falsely claiming resolution.

## Clusters and narratives
Clusters capture structure. They do not manufacture consensus. Competing structures may remain separate and attributable.

## Scoped Truth
Truth is congealed composition under a consumer policy. It preserves the series, clusters, Discoveries, confidence/stability information, and other attributable structures exposed by its contracts. Scoped Truth may be true, false, incomplete, or misleading relative to Consumer Reality.

## Explicit transfer
Knowledge remains local until explicitly transferred. A source Truth may become another entity's Evidence without becoming that entity's Truth automatically.

## Consequence
Consequence projection answers what available knowledge says may happen. Agency remains downstream.
