# Wilds.Discoveries Getting Started

Wilds.Discoveries turns legitimately available information into structured, attributable, evolving knowledge without teaching the platform what the consumer's domain means.

## Canonical progression

**Consumer Reality → Evidence → Proof → Facts → Discoveries → Knowledge Geometry / Momentum → Clusters → Scoped Truths → Consequences**

## Evidence and Proof

Evidence belongs to an exact entity and records an admitted observation or information item. Proof validates exact Evidence and may provide independent conclusions such as truth and believability. Proof does not choose which Fact the Evidence should support.

## Facts

Facts retain Evidence historically while an `IEvidenceContributionPolicy` decides which validated Evidence currently counts toward that Fact's resolution.

Use `CurrentEvidenceCount`, `EvidencePercentage`, and `IsProven` for current resolution state. Do not assume every retained Evidence item contributes.

## Discoveries

A Discovery becomes available from proven Facts according to its configured threshold. Unresolved Discoveries may still participate in vectorization and geometric development without pretending to be resolved.

## Geometry and motion

Consumer-defined dimensions describe the geometry. Discoveries preserves contributions, vectors, evaluations, ordered series, movement, persistence, and recognition without assigning semantic meaning to the dimensions.

## Narratives and Truth

Recognized geometry can form separate or competing narrative groups. Consumer composition and congealment policies decide what those structures mean and whether a scoped Truth may form.

A scoped Truth is not automatically Consumer Reality.

## Consequences

Consumer consequence-projection policy can derive what may result from a congealed Truth.

**Discoveries stops there. A Consequence is not an action.**

## Social influence

A group or other knowledge context can congeal a Truth. That exact Truth may then be explicitly admitted as new Evidence belonging to another entity. The receiving entity's policy decides whether it contributes. Ownership and history do not silently merge.
