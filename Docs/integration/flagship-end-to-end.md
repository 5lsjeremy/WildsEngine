# Flagship End-to-End Guide

The flagship stack combines simulation, programmable state processing, and attributable knowledge development without requiring a mandatory agency system.

## Processing path

1. Consumer-owned objects and runtime state enter through explicit boundaries.
2. QSL may process legitimately available state inside a Wilds-owned lifecycle.
3. Consumer Reality may be admitted as entity-owned Evidence.
4. Proof validates exact Evidence.
5. Consumer contribution policy determines which Evidence supports a Fact.
6. Proven Facts make Discoveries available.
7. Discoveries become consumer-defined vectors and ordered evaluations.
8. Geometry can persist and recognize structures.
9. Narrative composition preserves reinforcing or competing structures.
10. Consumer congealment policy may produce a scoped Truth.
11. Consumer consequence policy may project Consequences.
12. **STOP. Agency is downstream.**

## Kid 5: competing knowledge

Four nearby children choosing chocolate can support Kid 5's knowledge that chocolate is socially dominant while Kid 5 simultaneously retains a personal vanilla preference. Those propositions do not need to overwrite one another.

The certified path carries both through Evidence, Facts, Discoveries, vectorization, geometry, competing narratives, scoped Truth, and a projected Consequence without choosing an ice-cream flavor for Kid 5.

## Group Truth to individual Evidence

A separate knowledge context can congeal a group Truth such as "Chocolate is dominant among Kids 1-4." Producing that Truth does not modify Kid 5.

The consumer may explicitly create new Kid-5-owned Evidence whose outcome references that exact source Truth. One Kid 5 policy may accept the group Truth as peer pressure while another rejects its contribution. The source Truth and group history remain separate.

This is social influence without automatic conformity.

## Boundary

The flagship can expose what state is available, what knowledge has formed, what remains unresolved, what narratives compete, what has congealed into scoped Truth, and what Consequences are projected.

It does not decide what the entity should do next.
