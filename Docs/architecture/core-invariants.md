# Wilds Architecture — Core Invariants

This document is the concise 1.0 architecture canon.

## Consumer ownership

Consumer applications own domain vocabulary, state, policies, and semantic meaning. Domain-aware consumer policies are expected. Hidden domain-aware platform semantics are not.

## QSL

QSL is standalone stateful processing. Native properties of bound consumer objects are available immediately. Additional runtime vocabulary becomes available only through explicit state attachment. Unavailable properties remain unresolved.

## Wilds Core

Wilds Core is domain-neutral simulation infrastructure and established canon. Discoveries does not reshape Core for convenience. Any Core change requires a separate architectural decision.

## Discoveries

Canonical progression:

**Consumer Reality → Evidence → Proof → Facts → Discoveries → Knowledge Geometry / Momentum → Clusters → Scoped Truths → Consequences**

Authorization or observation may describe how Evidence is admitted. Neither is a separate canonical knowledge object.

### Epistemic separation

Evidence is not automatically a Fact. A Fact is not automatically every entity's knowledge. Partial Discovery resolution can have trajectory without claiming completion. Clustering identifies structure, not consensus. Scoped Truth is congealed composition, not universal reality.

### Knowledge transfer

Knowledge remains local until explicitly transferred. A scoped Truth from one knowledge context may become new Evidence owned by another entity through an explicit attributable transition. The source Truth, history, series, clusters, and Discoveries do not transfer ownership. The receiving entity's policies decide whether the new Evidence contributes.

### Consequence

A Consequence describes what may result from available knowledge under consumer-supplied meaning and policy. It does not select or execute an action.

**Consequence projection is the current product boundary.**

## Authoring

Remove mechanical paperwork when safe. Do not hide semantic responsibility. Generated identities and safe defaults are appropriate only where they do not weaken identity, provenance, scope, or consumer meaning.

## Failure

When required state, vocabulary, identity, provenance, authority, or knowledge is missing, prefer explicit unresolved or failed results over invention.

## Provenance

At every legitimate transition: **preserve identity, preserve provenance, preserve consumer ownership, preserve knowledge boundaries.**

## Architectural test

Ask:

1. Where did this meaning come from?
2. Who is entitled to know it?
3. Does it belong inside the locked product scope?

If the answer becomes “Wilds just knew,” the boundary has failed.
